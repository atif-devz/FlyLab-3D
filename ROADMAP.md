# FlyLab 3D roadmap

1. **Research workbench.** Federated source search, linked experiment design records and statistical inference. Verify the existing adult and larval workflows alongside the new release.
2. **Life-stage anatomy.** Review candidates for licensed embryo, larval, pupal and adult geometry. Record version, species, stage, sex, source, license and transforms for each asset. Test registration against reference anatomy and retain clear schematic labels for current models.
3. **Spatial data and additional labs.** Evaluate cited connectome and expression visualizations and expand bench coverage with independent Drosophila reviewers. Attribute every observation and identify the life stage and expected outcome of new training tasks.
4. **Research and teaching workflows.** Prioritize dataset interoperability, repeated-measures and survival inference, richer classroom assessment, and verified immersive support. Review storage, permissions and accessibility before offering shared institutional data services.

See the [design and validation brief](docs/Design_and_Validation.md) for existing release and review requirements.

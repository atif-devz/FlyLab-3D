# Code of Conduct

FlyLab 3D welcomes participation from learners, educators, researchers, and developers. Treat people with respect regardless of their identity, background, institution, or level of experience.

## Community standards

- Discuss methods, evidence, and contributions on their merits. Make criticism specific and constructive.
- Be patient with questions and disagreements. Acknowledge corrections and credit other people's work.
- Protect personal information, including unpublished affiliations and private communications.
- Keep project spaces free of harassment, threats, discrimination, intimidation, and retaliation.

These standards apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where participants represent the project.

## Reporting and moderation

For conduct on GitHub, use GitHub's reporting controls or a private moderation contact published by the repository owner. Include links and enough context to identify what happened. Keep private details out of public issues and comments.

Maintainers may remove abusive content, limit participation, or block accounts when needed. Technical bugs and scientific disagreements have dedicated issue templates. Use the [security policy](SECURITY.md) for security reports.
